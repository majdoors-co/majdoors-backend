Our Story

Founded by Ravi Kumar, Majdoors Mart started with a simple vision: to make high-quality construction and interior materials accessible and affordable to everyone in Bihar Sharif and surrounding areas.

We understand that building a home or a commercial space is a significant investment. That's why we bring together a comprehensive catalog of products—from electrical supplies to premium flooring—under one roof.

But we didn't stop there. We also recognized the challenge of finding reliable professionals. So, we created a platform to connect you with verified skilled workers like electricians, plumbers, painters, and more.
